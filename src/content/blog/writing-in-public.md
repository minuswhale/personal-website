I have ten years of private notes and, apparently, nothing to show for them. That's not quite true — the notes made *me* — but nobody else ever got to use them.

## The half-thought problem

A private note is allowed to be a dead end. Mine are full of half-thoughts: "look into how V8 strings work??" followed by nothing. Writing in public forces the second half. If I can't finish the thought for a reader, I didn't have the thought.

## The strange-mailbox effect

The other thing: every time I've published something genuinely odd — a deep dive into terminal fonts, a post about deleting code — someone has emailed me within a week to say *they think about that too*. Private notes never once did that.

## The rules I'm setting

1. Write the post I wish existed, not the post that performs.
2. Small and finished beats big and drafted.
3. No apologizing for gaps between posts. This is a desk, not a publication schedule.

That's the whole policy. Let's see if it survives contact with a busy quarter.
